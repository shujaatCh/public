Privacy Policy App: Quit Smoking: Smoke Free Tracker Effective date: October 23, 2026 In short: all your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. The app works fully offline and needs no account. 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how "Quit Smoking: Smoke Free Tracker" ("the App"), developed by Shujaat Ismaeel ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy. 2. Information We Collect We do not collect personal information on any server. The information you enter in the App, such as your quit date, cigarettes per day, pack price, currency, reasons for quitting, cravings, triggers, mood, journal notes, symptoms, goals, contacts and optional photos, is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not uploaded anywhere. 3. How Your Data Is Used Data you enter is used only inside the App to show your smoke-free time, savings, statistics, charts, reminders and achievements. 4. Permissions the App May Request Notifications and exact alarms: to deliver reminders and milestone alerts you choose to turn on. Physical activity (optional): only if you enable the step counter. Step data stays on your device. Photos / media (optional): only if you add a picture to your reasons, goals or journal. Pictures stay on your device. Biometrics / device lock (optional): to protect the App with a PIN or fingerprint. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessed by the App. Phone / SMS (optional): the Support Circle buttons open your phone's dialer or messaging app. The App does not read your contacts' data or send messages automatically. You can deny or revoke any permission in your device settings. The related feature will then be unavailable. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone. Data leaves your device only when you choose to export a backup, create a PDF report, or share a progress image through your device's share menu. What you do with those files is up to you. 6. Advertising (if enabled) Some versions of the App may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). If the version you installed has no ads, this section does not apply. 7. Data Storage, Backup and Deletion Your data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App or use "Reset Data" in Settings, your data is permanently deleted. Backups you export are saved where you choose, so please keep them safe. If your device backs up app data through Android or Google, that backup is managed by your device settings, not by us. 8. Security We keep data on your device and offer an optional app lock (PIN or biometrics). No method of storage is completely secure, so please also protect your device with a screen lock. 9. Children's Privacy The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. 10. Health Disclaimer This App is a self-help tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition and does not replace professional medical advice. Health milestone information is a general estimate. Please consult a doctor or qualified healthcare provider before starting any quitting aid, nicotine replacement or medication. 11.
