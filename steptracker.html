Privacy Policy Step Tracker – Pedometer & Activity Tracker Effective date: October 29, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how the Step Tracker app (“the app”, “we”, “our”) handles information. The app is developed by Shujaat Ismaeel. We built the app to be free and private: no account, no login, and no advertising. 1. Information we do not collect We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that receive your data, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. 2. Information the app uses on your device To work properly, the app uses the following data. It is processed and stored locally on your device only: Step and activity data: your phone’s motion sensor is used to count steps and calculate distance, calories, and activity history shown in graphs and logs. Location data (optional): if you start GPS route tracking, the app uses your device’s location to draw your route on a map and measure distance. Location is used only while you track an activity and is stored on your device. Goals and health entries: your step goals, achievements, water intake, workout entries, and reminder settings are stored locally. 3. Permissions Physical activity (activity recognition): to count your steps. Location: for the real-time map tracker. You can deny it and still use step counting. Notifications: to show goal progress and health reminders. Foreground service: to keep step counting running in the background with low battery use. You can change or revoke any permission at any time in your device settings. 4. Google Fit / Health Connect (optional) If you choose to sync with Google Fit or Health Connect, the app reads or writes your activity data with your permission. That data is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive a copy of it. You can disconnect syncing at any time in the app or in your Google account settings. 5. Third-party services The map feature may use Google Maps services, which can process location and device information according to Google’s Privacy Policy. The app does not contain ads, analytics trackers, or other third-party advertising services. 6. Data storage and security Your data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored by the app. Because we do not hold your data on a server, we cannot recover it if you delete the app or lose your device. 7. Children’s privacy The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. 9.
