Ask Pakistan (“the app”, “we”, “us”) lets you ask questions about Pakistan and get answers with source links. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. By using the app you agree to this policy. In short: the app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, CNIC or location. The questions you type are sent to our server so we can answer them. Information we collect Questions you type or speak. The text of your question and recent messages in the same conversation (so follow-up questions make sense) are sent to our server to produce an answer. Feedback reports. If you tap “Report wrong answer”, we receive the question, the answer and any note you add. Technical data. Our server may log your IP address, app version, Android version, device model, language and request time. We use this to prevent abuse and fix errors. Crash and performance data. If we enable a crash reporting tool, it may collect anonymous diagnostic information. Please do not type personal or sensitive details (such as CNIC numbers, passwords, bank details or phone numbers) into the question box. Information stored on your device Saved answers, saved guides, question history and checklist progress are stored locally on your phone. Your settings (language, theme, notification and voice options) are stored locally. You can delete this data at any time from Settings > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Permissions Internet: required to send questions and receive answers. Microphone (optional): used only if you choose voice input. Speech is converted to text by Android’s speech recognition service, which is provided by your device or Google and is covered by their privacy policies. We do not store audio. Notifications (optional): used for checklist reminders and the daily “Did you know?” fact. You can turn these off in Settings or in Android settings. How we use information To generate answers to your questions. To improve answer quality, fix wrong answers and add missing sources. To protect the service from spam and abuse. To keep the app working and secure. We do not sell your data. We do not use your questions for advertising. Third-party services To answer questions, the app relies on service providers that may process your question text on our behalf: An AI language model provider, to write answers. A web search provider, for questions about current information such as news, prices or deadlines. Cloud hosting and database providers, to run our server. Android system services (such as speech recognition and Chrome Custom Tabs for opening links). These providers only receive the information needed to perform their service. When you open a source link, you leave the app and the privacy policy of that website applies. Data retention Server logs and feedback reports are kept only as long as needed to run, secure and improve the service, and are then deleted or anonymized. Cached answers do not include personal information. Security Data is sent between the app and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Children’s privacy The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it. Not a government app Ask Pakistan is an independent information app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any government of Pakistan department or agency. Answers may contain errors, so always verify important details, fees and legal requirements on the official website. The app does not provide legal, medical or financial advice. Your choices Clear your saved data and history from Settings at any time. Turn off notifications and voice features at any time. You can ask us to delete any feedback report or question you submitted, by emailing us from the address below with the details. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
