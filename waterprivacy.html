Privacy Policy for Water Reminder Effective Date: October 19, 2026 Last Updated: October 19, 2026 Thank you for using Water Reminder ("the App"). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your information when you download, install, and use the Water Reminder mobile application. 1. Overview (Privacy First) No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your name, email address, physical location, or any identifying details. 100% On-Device Storage: All your hydration records, daily goals, cup sizes, and reminder configurations are stored strictly on your local device. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising: The app contains zero advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party data brokers. No Cloud Synchronization or Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or connect any social profile to use the App. 2. Information We Store Locally on Your Device The App stores the following data locally on your device solely to deliver hydration tracking: Hydration Logs: Timestamps, beverage type (Water, Tea, Juice, Other), and amount drank. User Preferences: Daily water goal, preferred reminder interval, active reminder hours, and measurement units (glasses, ml, or oz). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. 3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used Water Reminder requests only standard Android permissions necessary for local operation: Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly to send local reminders to drink water at your chosen intervals. Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger your hydration reminders on time throughout your active hours. Run at Startup (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to restore your scheduled reminder alarms after your device is restarted. Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide gentle tactile feedback when logging a drink and alerting you to reminders. The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location. 4. Third-Party Services Water Reminder does not use third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics) or advertising networks. The app operates completely offline. 5. Data Retention and Deletion Because your data is stored locally on your device: You can clear your daily logs at any time inside the App under Settings > More options > Clear today's drinks. You can export your data as a CSV file anytime via Settings > More options > Export data. You can permanently erase all data at any time by clearing the App's storage via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Water Reminder > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the App. 6. Children's Privacy The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or adults alike, making it suitable and safe for all ages. 7.
