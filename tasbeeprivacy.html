Tasbeeh - Dhikr Counter does not collect, track, or share any personal or sensitive user data. All records are stored 100% locally on your personal device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data or sensitive information:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not request, collect, or store your name, email, phone number, or ID.
  • No Account Required: No login, signup, or user registration is required to use the app.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access GPS or network location.
  • No Media/Contacts Access: The app does not access photos, contacts, camera, or microphone.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All recitation counters, custom supplications, and session logs are saved exclusively in your device's internal storage:

  • Dhikr counts and target goals (e.g. 33, 100)
  • Personal custom dhikrs created by you
  • Daily recitation streaks and weekly activity history
  • Sound and vibration preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not synced to external cloud servers. You can reset it anytime from within the app.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback on count taps and target goals.
  • Screen Wake Lock: Used optionally to prevent the screen from turning off while reciting.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics tracking tools, profiling cookies, or data brokers. No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

The application is safe and appropriate for users of all ages. We do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Security

Your data is protected by your device's native operating system security sandbox because it is never sent across the internet.
