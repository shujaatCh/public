# Privacy Policy **Age Calculator: Date of Birth** Last updated: October 17, 2026 **In short:** the dates of birth and names you enter stay on your device. We don't run accounts, we don't collect your personal details on our servers, and we never sell your data. The only data that leaves your device is limited advertising and device information handled by Google AdMob. This Privacy Policy explains how **Shujaat Ismaeel** ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app **Age Calculator: Date of Birth** (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy. ## 1. Information you enter To calculate ages, the App lets you enter dates of birth and, optionally, names, relations and photos or emoji for saved people. This information is stored **locally on your device only**. We do not upload it, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it. ## 2. Information collected automatically The App itself does not require registration, location, contacts, microphone or camera access. However, our advertising partner may automatically collect the following to serve and measure ads: - **Advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, app interactions:** used for showing, limiting and measuring ads, and for fraud prevention (Google AdMob). - **Crash and performance diagnostics (if enabled):** used for fixing bugs and improving stability (Google / Firebase). Learn how Google handles this data in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and at How Google uses data from partner apps (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). ## 3. Advertising and consent The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade removes ads. ## 4. Notifications If you enable birthday reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the App or in Android settings. ## 5. Sharing When you tap Share, the App prepares text or an image and hands it to Android's share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) and is governed by that app's privacy policy. We don't receive the shared content. ## 6. In-app purchases Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was completed, never your payment details. ## 7. Third-party services - Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Google Play Services / Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 8. Data retention and deletion Because your entries live only on your device, you control them: delete individual people inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove everything. For data held by Google (such as advertising data), use your Google account and Android ad settings, or contact us and we will help point you to the right place. ## 9. Children's privacy The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will assist. ## 10. Security Data in transit between the App and ad services is encrypted. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. ## 11. Your rights Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Since we hold no personal data about you, most of these are exercised directly on your device. For questions or requests relating to advertising data, contact us below. ## 12. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be noted in the App or on this page. ## 13.
