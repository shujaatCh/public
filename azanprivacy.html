PRIVACY POLICY FOR AZAN ALARAM Last updated: October 15, 2026 Azan Alaram ("the App", "we", "our") is an Islamic prayer times and Azan alarm app for Android. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled. 1. SUMMARY - We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. - The App works completely offline. - The App contains no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics. - Your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times. 2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or any other personal information. We do not require you to create an account. 3. LOCATION INFORMATION If you tap "Get Current Location", the App uses your device's location (coordinates) to find your nearest city and calculate accurate prayer times. - Location is processed only on your device. - It is not sent to our servers or to any third party. - We do not store your location history. - Location permission is optional. You can instead select your city manually. 4. APP PERMISSIONS The App may request the following permissions, used only for the purposes below: - Location (approximate/precise): To detect your city and calculate prayer times. - Notifications: To show prayer time reminders and Azan alerts. - Exact alarms / Schedule alarms: To play the Azan at the exact prayer time. - Run at startup (boot completed): To restore your Azan alarms after your device restarts. - Wake lock / Vibrate: To make sure the alarm sounds reliably. 5. SETTINGS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE Your preferences (selected city, language, juristic method, Azan sound, mosque time adjustments, and alarm on/off choices) are saved locally on your device only. They are never uploaded or shared, and are removed when you uninstall the App. 6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with any third party. 7. INTERNET USE The App does not need an internet connection to calculate prayer times or play Azan alarms. 8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for users of all ages. 9. DATA SECURITY Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data on our side to be exposed. Your settings remain private on your own device. 10. YOUR RIGHTS Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can erase all locally stored settings at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. 11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy. 12.
